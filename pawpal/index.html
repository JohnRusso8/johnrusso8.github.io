Getting started

  1. Open PawPal and tap Create poses with AI.
  2. Tap Choose a photo of your pet and pick a clear, well-lit photo where your pet faces the camera. Your first portrait is free and takes about 15 seconds.
  3. Choose poses (smiling, head tilt, napping and more) and tap Create. Each pose uses one credit and comes with one free retry.

Tip: the best results come from a close-up photo with good light, both eyes visible, and nothing covering your pet's face.

Adding widgets

Touch and hold an empty spot on your Home Screen, tap Edit → Add Widget, search for PawPal, and choose PawPal 1, 2 or 3 in any size. Small widgets change about every 10–15 seconds; medium and large widgets change every couple of seconds and show all your info lines.

Choose what appears under your pet (speech bubble, greeting, weather, next event, ride timer) on PawPal's main screen under Under your pet.

Adding PawPal to CarPlay

  1. On your iPhone, open Settings → General → CarPlay and choose your car.
  2. Tap Widgets → Add Widgets and add PawPal 1 (or 2 or 3).
  3. Drag it to the top, and turn off Smart Rotate so your pet stays on screen.

Your car needs to have connected to your iPhone at least once, and CarPlay widgets require iOS 26. While your iPhone is locked, CarPlay updates widgets about once a minute. See also Apple's guide: Customize widgets in CarPlay.

More than one pet

Tap Add a pet on the main screen. You can add the pet to an existing widget, where your pets take turns, or give it a new widget of its own (up to three). Each new pet's portrait uses one credit. You can rename a pet, move it to another widget, or remove it from its page.

Deleted poses aren't gone right away: open your pet's page, then Deleted, to restore them or delete them permanently.

Credits and purchases

  • Credits come in packs of 1, 3 or 5. Tap the bag button on the Create with AI screen to buy more.
  • A credit is only used when a pose is created successfully. If something goes wrong, you aren't charged.
  • Credits don't expire and have no cash value.
  • If a purchase went through but your credits didn't appear, close and reopen PawPal; they're added automatically.

Refunds

Refunds are handled by Apple. In PawPal, open Create with AI → Get more poses → Purchase history & refunds and tap Request refund, or visit reportaproblem.apple.com. While Apple reviews a request, that purchase's credits are on hold. If the refund is approved, its unused credits are removed; poses you already created stay.

New iPhone, restoring, and deleting your account

  • Keep your pets on a new iPhone: on PawPal's main screen, tap Sign in with Apple. On your new iPhone, sign in the same way and tap Restore my pet. PawPal doesn't receive your name or email.
  • Without signing in, your pets and credits are tied to this iPhone and may be lost if you delete the app.
  • Delete your account: main screen → Account → Delete account & pet data. This permanently deletes your pets' images, your credits and your history from PawPal.
