PawPal Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 2, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how PawPal ("the App," "we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and shares information when you use the App and its widgets on iPhone and CarPlay.

The short version: PawPal doesn't ask for your name or email, doesn't show ads, and doesn't track you. Your pet's photo is only sent to our servers when you choose to create poses, and most of your settings never leave your iPhone.

1. Information We Collect

Anonymous Account

  • When you use the AI features, the App creates an anonymous account through Firebase Authentication. It's a random ID that isn't linked to your name, email, or Apple Account. We use it to keep track of your pose credits and your pet's images.

Your Pet's Photo and Generated Images

  • When you choose a photo to create your pet's portrait, that photo is uploaded to our servers (Google Firebase) and sent to Google's Gemini image service to create the portrait and poses.
  • We store the photo you uploaded, your pet's portrait, and the poses you create on our servers, linked to your anonymous account.
  • The App only receives the photos you select. It never has access to the rest of your photo library.
  • Please only upload photos of your pet. Don't upload photos that show people.

Purchases

  • Payments are handled entirely by Apple. We never see your payment details.
  • When you buy pose credits, Apple gives us details about the transaction (such as a transaction ID, the product, and the date), which we store with your anonymous account to add your credits and prevent the same purchase from being used twice.

Credit and Usage Records

  • We store your credit balance and a record of the poses you've created (which pose, when, and whether its free retry was used).

Information That Stays on Your iPhone

  • Pet name and settings: stored only on your device.
  • Location (optional): if you turn on the weather line, the App uses your approximate location to request the forecast directly from Apple Weather. Your location is not sent to our servers.
  • Calendar (optional): if you turn on the next-event line, the App reads your upcoming events on your device to show in the widget. Calendar data is never sent to our servers.

Information We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, or physical address.
  • We do not show ads, and we do not use advertising identifiers or track you across apps and websites.
  • We do not use third-party analytics.

2. How We Use Information

  • To create your pet's portrait and poses and show them in the App and widgets.
  • To add, track, and use your pose credits.
  • To prevent fraud and abuse, such as reusing a purchase or using credits from a purchase that is being refunded.
  • To fix problems and keep the service running.

3. Sharing with Apple for Refund Requests

If you ask Apple for a refund of a purchase made in PawPal, Apple may ask us how much of the purchase was used. We share information such as whether the purchase was delivered and how many of its pose credits were used. Apple uses this to help review your request. By making a purchase in PawPal, you agree to this sharing.

4. Service Providers

Google Firebase (Google LLC)

  • Firebase Authentication (anonymous accounts), Cloud Firestore (credits and records), Cloud Storage (your pet's images), and Cloud Functions (our server).
  • Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Google Gemini API (Google LLC)

  • Creates your pet's portrait and poses from your photo. We use the paid Gemini API, which Google's terms say isn't used to improve Google's products.
  • Gemini API Terms: https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/terms

Apple

  • Processes purchases and refunds (App Store) and provides weather data (Apple Weather).
  • Privacy Policy: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

5. Data Retention and Deletion

We keep your anonymous account, credit balance, purchase records, and pet images for as long as you use the App. Deleting the App removes everything stored on your iPhone, but not the images and records on our servers. To have your server data deleted, email us from the contact below and include that you use PawPal; we'll delete your pet's images and account data. We may keep minimal purchase records where needed to prevent fraud or meet legal requirements.

6. Children's Privacy

PawPal is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will delete it.

7. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, or delete your information, or to object to how it is used. To make a request, contact us at the email below.

8. International Users

Your information is processed and stored in the United States. By using the App, you consent to this transfer.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Effective Date" above shows the latest revision. Continued use of the App after changes means you accept the updated policy.
