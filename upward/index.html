Upward

Social fitness: a training plan that adapts to you, nutrition targets that follow your progress, and friends who keep you going.

What Upward does

  • A plan built for you. Answer a few questions and Upward picks a strength plan for your goal, schedule and equipment, with warm-ups, weight suggestions and a review at the end of each block.
  • Calories and macros that adjust. Set a goal weight and pace. Upward sets your calories, protein, carbs and fat, and suggests changes when your weigh-ins show you're off pace.
  • Fast food logging. Search foods, scan barcodes, or paste notes and let Upward read them.
  • Train with friends. Follow people, see their workouts, like, comment and send a nudge.

Accounts and signing in

  • You can use Upward without signing in. Your data is then tied to this phone.
  • Sign in with Apple, Google or your phone number from the Profile tab to keep your data safe and use it on another phone.
  • Each sign-in method is its own account. To get your data back, sign in the same way you did before.
  • If you used Upward signed out and then sign in to an account you already have, what you logged while signed out is moved into that account.

Deleting your account

In Upward, open Profile, scroll to the bottom and tap Delete account. This permanently deletes your account and everything in it: your goal, weigh-ins, food logs, workouts, supplements, photos, profile, posts, likes, comments and follows. It can't be undone.

Blocking and reporting

On anyone's profile, open the menu to Block or Report them. Someone you block can't see your profile or interact with you. Reports are reviewed, and accounts that break our Terms are restricted or removed.
