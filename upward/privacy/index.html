Upward Privacy Policy

Effective October 20, 2026

This policy explains what the Upward app (“Upward”, “we”) collects, why, who it's shared with, and how to delete it. Upward does not sell your data and does not show ads.

What we collect

Your account

  • If you sign in with Apple or Google, the email address they share with us. Apple lets you hide your email, in which case we get a private relay address.
  • If you sign in with your phone number, that number.
  • If you don't sign in, an anonymous account ID tied to your phone.

What you enter

  • Goal and body details: your goal, current and goal weight, pace, height, age, sex, training experience and days, and any calorie or macro targets you set.
  • Weigh-ins and the trend calculated from them.
  • Food logs, including notes you ask Upward to read.
  • Supplements and the days you took them.
  • Workouts: your plan, the sets, weights and reps you log, notes, and exercises you mark as having hurt.
  • Exercise names you add. These are also offered to a shared exercise library, which other users see only after we review and approve them.
  • Photos you take or choose: progress photos and photos you post.
  • Social activity: your profile name and the location text you type (never your phone's location), who you follow and block, posts, likes, comments, nudges, and reports you make.

Collected automatically

  • A push notification token, so we can send the notifications you've turned on.
  • Which features you use, such as that a food was logged by barcode or a workout was finished, plus basic app information like app opens, app version and device type. This is collected with Google Analytics for Firebase and never includes what you log, your weight, your messages, or who you follow. You can turn it off in Profile under Share anonymous usage stats.

What other people can see

Private by default and never shown to others: your weight, body details, weigh-ins, food logs, calorie and macro targets, progress photos, notes, and account email or phone number.

If you create a profile, other signed-in Upward users can see your profile name and location text, plus the sections you choose to share: your goal's name (for example “Build muscle”), your weekly split, your finished workouts and streak, your supplement stack, and photos you post. You can hide any of these in your profile settings. Likes and comments you leave are visible to people who can see the post.

Apple Health

If you choose to, Upward reads the calories, protein, carbs and fat that other apps have saved to Apple Health, to show your daily nutrition. This data is read on your phone and used only to show your totals in the app. Upward does not send Apple Health data to its servers, does not write to Apple Health, and never uses it for advertising or shares it with anyone.

Services we use

  • Google Firebase stores your account and data, sends notifications and provides app analytics. See Firebase privacy.
  • Apple and Google handle Sign in with Apple and Google Sign-In, and Apple delivers notifications to iPhones.
  • USDA FoodData Central receives the food names you search for.
  • Open Food Facts receives the barcode numbers you scan.
  • NIH Dietary Supplement Label Database receives supplement names you search for.
  • Anthropic receives the text of food notes you ask Upward to read (signed-in accounts only), to turn them into food entries. It is not sent your name, email or other data.

Food, barcode and supplement searches don't include your name or account details. Exercise demo links open on YouTube, which has its own privacy policy.

How we use your data

  • To run the app: build your plan, calculate your targets, suggest weights and adjustments, and keep your data in sync across your phones.
  • To run the social features you choose to use, and to send notifications you've turned on.
  • To keep the community safe: reviewing reports and restricting accounts that break our Terms. When someone is reported, we keep a copy of the reported content for review.
  • To understand which features are used, so we can improve them, and to fix problems. You can switch this off in Profile.

We don't sell your data, use it for advertising, or share it except as described above or when required by law.

Keeping and deleting your data

We keep your data while you have an account. To delete it, open Profile in Upward and tap Delete account. This permanently deletes your account, your private data and photos, your profile, posts, likes, comments and follows, reports made by or about you, and your notification tokens. Exercise names you suggested to the shared library may stay there, without anything that identifies you.

If you used Upward without signing in and then sign in to an existing account, the data from that signed-out session is moved into the account and the anonymous account is deleted.

You can also ask us to delete your data, or for a copy of it, by emailing us.

Security

Your data is sent over encrypted connections and stored with Google Firebase. Access rules let only you read your private data. No method of storage is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information.

Children

Upward is not meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child under 13 has an account, contact us and we'll delete it.

Changes and contact

If we change this policy, we'll update the date above and, for significant changes, tell you in the app.
